Import your data

One-time step: this copies everything saved in this browser (themes, words, journal entries, conjugation tables, everything) into your logged-in account, so it follows you to any device from now on.

Safe to run more than once — it only fills in things your account doesn't already have, never overwrites anything you've already saved since switching to accounts.
